# Commercial use

SE Inspect (formerly VORSA INSPECT), VORSA-M3 and the accompanying tools are published under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (see `LICENSE`). In short:

- **Free** for personal use, research, teaching, hobby projects, evaluation, and for non-profit organisations — including modification and redistribution under the same terms.
- **Not covered**: using the software, or anything derived from it, for or on behalf of a commercial enterprise, or in a product or service that is sold.

If you want to use SE Inspect commercially — in a factory, as part of a product, as a paid service, or inside a company — you need a separate agreement. That is deliberately simple: write to **hello@spikingedge.com** with

1. who you are and what you want to inspect,
2. how many cells / cards / sites,
3. whether you need support, adaptations or a guarantee.

You will get a plain answer and, where it fits, a plain licence. Early industrial users who share measurements back into the public evidence pages are very welcome.

Why this model: the project is built by one person in the open, and the published numbers are only worth something if the work can continue. Non-commercial use costs nothing; commercial use finances the next release.
